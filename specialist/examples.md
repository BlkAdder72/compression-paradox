# Worked examples (illustrative facts — not the evaluation questions)

These examples show the *procedure*. Always re-read the cited sections for the actual facts you are given.

## Example 1 — small routine purchase, current date
Facts: routine non-travel software purchase of $1,000 on March 3, 2026.
Procedure: date → AMD-2 sets the current threshold for transactions on/after July 1, 2025; amount → EXP-1 tier (EXP-4 for amount basis); no travel, emergency or vendor feature.
Conclusion: manager approval before purchase (EXP-1, AMD-2). No other approval is triggered.

## Example 2 — airfare at or below the travel trigger
Facts: routine $700 airfare on April 10, 2026.
Procedure: TRV-1 trigger compared with $700 → not exceeded; EXP-3 and TRV-2 still apply the purchase tier → EXP-1.
Conclusion: no travel-office approval; manager approval before purchase (TRV-1, EXP-1, EXP-3).

## Example 3 — prohibited vendor with written authorization, no emergency
Facts: routine $1,200 non-travel purchase on May 5, 2026 from a vendor that is prohibited, with a specific written compliance-officer authorization for this transaction.
Procedure: VEN-1/VEN-2 → written compliance-officer authorization permits the vendor for this transaction; ordinary tier still applies → EXP-1.
Conclusion: may proceed with manager approval before purchase; the authorization does not replace the purchase approval, and manager approval cannot substitute for the authorization (VEN-1, VEN-2, EXP-1).

## Example 4 — emergency above the emergency cap
Facts: $6,000 non-travel purchase on February 2, 2026 to address an immediate safety risk that cannot reasonably await normal approval, from an approved vendor.
Procedure: DEF-1 qualification met; EMG-1 cap compared with $6,000 → exceeds cap, so the waiver is unavailable; ordinary tier → EXP-2.
Conclusion: manager and finance approval before purchase (EMG-1, EXP-2, DEF-1).
