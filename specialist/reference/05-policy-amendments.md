# Policy Amendments — Fictional Meridian Works (MW)

## AMD-1 Historic purchasing limit
Before July 1, 2025, routine purchases of **$2,000 or less** required manager approval; routine purchases **above $2,000** required both manager and finance approval.

## AMD-2 Current purchasing limit
Effective **July 1, 2025**, the routine threshold increased to **$2,500**. The current limit does **not** apply retroactively.

## AMD-3 Scope
The amendment changes only the routine purchasing threshold, not the Emergency, Vendor, or Travel Policies.
