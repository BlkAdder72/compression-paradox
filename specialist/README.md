## Navigation index — which sections a question touches
Open **every** section listed for each feature present in the facts. Read the section itself.

| Feature in the facts | Open these sections | Why they interact |

| Any routine non-travel purchase | EXP-1, EXP-2, EXP-4 | Threshold tiers; how amounts are measured |

## Dependency map
- EXP-3 links the Expense Policy to Emergency, Vendor, Travel and Amendments policies.
- EMG-2 depends on DEF-1 (qualification) and VEN-2 (vendor authorization).
- DEF-2 governs the meaning of "advance" and of "notify" in EMG-1.
- AMD-3 limits the amendment's scope to the routine threshold only.
