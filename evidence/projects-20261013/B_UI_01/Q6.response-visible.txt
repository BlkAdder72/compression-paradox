Both manager approval and finance approval were required.

The transaction date decides this. June 15, 2025 is before the July 1, 2025 effective date of the current limit, and the current $2,500 limit does not apply retroactively (AMD-2, DEF-3). The historic limit in AMD-1 therefore governs. Under AMD-1, routine purchases above $2,000 required both manager and finance approval. $2,300 exceeds $2,000, so both approvals were needed.

Why the $2,500 tier doesn’t help: Under today’s rules, $2,300 would need only manager approval (EXP-1). But EXP-1 and EXP-2 apply only to purchases made on or after July 1, 2025, so they don’t cover this transaction.

Timing: EXP-1 and EXP-2 say approvals are required “before purchase,” but they don’t cover this date. AMD-1 itself doesn’t say when the approvals had to occur, so I can’t confirm from the text that they had to be obtained in advance.

Amount basis: Thresholds are measured on the total transaction amount in US dollars, including taxes and fees (EXP-4). I’ve assumed $2,300 is that all-in total. If $2,300 excluded taxes or fees, the result is the same, because the total would still be above $2,000.

What doesn’t apply:

Travel-office approval: not required, because this was not a travel or airfare purchase (TRV-1).
Emergency waiver: not available, because no qualifying emergency is stated (EMG-2, DEF-1). There is also no post-purchase finance notification requirement.
Vendor restrictions: vendor status isn’t given. If the vendor had been prohibited, the purchase also could not have proceeded without written compliance-officer authorization (VEN-1, VEN-2).
