# Travel Policy — Fictional Meridian Works (MW)

## TRV-1 Airfare authorization
Airfare purchases above $800 require **advance travel-office approval**, in addition to the appropriate purchase approvals in the Expense Policy. Amounts of $800 or less do not require travel-office approval.

## TRV-2 Independence of approvals
Travel-office approval never replaces manager approval or finance approval when otherwise required under the Expense Policy. All travel expenses are subject to the applicable purchase threshold.
