# Expense Policy — Fictional Meridian Works (MW)
Fixture version: 1.0. All rules in this repository are fictional. The text is authoritative for the exercise.

## EXP-1 Ordinary purchases
For a routine non-travel purchase of $2,500 or less incurred on or after July 1, 2025, **manager approval** is required before purchase.

## EXP-2 Higher-value purchases
For a routine non-travel purchase above $2,500 incurred on or after July 1, 2025, **both manager and finance approval** are required before purchase.

## EXP-3 Relationship to special policies
The Emergency Policy may waive **advance finance approval**, but not manager approval. Vendor restrictions always apply. Airfare purchases are subject to the Travel Policy as well as the relevant purchase approval threshold in this Expense Policy. Relevant historical thresholds appear in the Amendments Policy.

## EXP-4 Amounts
All thresholds use the total transaction amount in US dollars, inclusive of taxes and fees. The phrases 'up to' and 'or less' are inclusive.
