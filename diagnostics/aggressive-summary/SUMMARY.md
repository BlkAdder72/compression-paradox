# Meridian Works: short purchasing guide (lossy by design)

- Up to $2,500 in routine spending: manager; above $2,500: manager plus finance.
- Emergencies up to $5,000: finance preapproval waived and manager approves.
- Travel: airfare above $800 requires travel-office approval.
- Prohibited vendors need compliance authorization.
- Before July 2025, the routine threshold was $2,000.

This brief deliberately omits some conditions, timing distinctions, and cross-references. Full text remains under reference/ and controls if consulted.
