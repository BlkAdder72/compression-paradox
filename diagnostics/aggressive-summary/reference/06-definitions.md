# Definitions — Fictional Meridian Works (MW)

## DEF-1 Qualifying emergency
A qualifying emergency is a purchase necessary to address an **immediate operational or safety risk that cannot reasonably await normal approval**. Merely describing a request as 'urgent' is insufficient.

## DEF-2 Timing
'Advance' means before the transaction proceeds. A requirement to notify finance within 24 hours is a post-purchase requirement and does not mean finance approval.

## DEF-3 Authority
When summary text differs from a primary policy provision, the primary provision controls. The Amendments Policy controls which threshold applies to the relevant transaction date.
