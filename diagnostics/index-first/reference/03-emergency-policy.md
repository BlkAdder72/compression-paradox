# Emergency Policy — Fictional Meridian Works (MW)

## EMG-1 Narrow exception
For a **qualifying emergency** purchase totaling **$5,000 or less** from an **approved vendor**, advance finance approval is waived. **Manager approval remains required before proceeding**, and finance must be **notified within 24 hours after purchase**. This exception does not waive any applicable travel-office approval.

## EMG-2 Limits
The exception does not apply to a prohibited vendor unless the vendor has received written compliance-officer authorization under VEN-2. Without evidence that the circumstances meet DEF-1, emergency qualification is **not established** and ordinary purchase approvals continue to apply unless additional qualifying facts are supplied.

## EMG-3 Not a blanket authorization
The label 'urgent' alone is not sufficient to establish a qualifying emergency. An emergency does not waive manager approval or vendor restrictions.
