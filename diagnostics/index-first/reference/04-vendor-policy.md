# Vendor Policy — Fictional Meridian Works (MW)

## VEN-1 Prohibited vendors
A purchase from a prohibited vendor **must not proceed** without written compliance-officer authorization. Emergency status alone does not change this prohibition.

## VEN-2 Exception authorization
A specific written authorization by the compliance officer permits use of an otherwise prohibited vendor for the approved transaction. No such authorization may be inferred from manager or finance approval.

## VEN-3 Approved vendors
An approved vendor is one designated approved in the transaction facts. The question set explicitly identifies vendor status when relevant.
